# CS301: Introduction to Machine Learning — Course Syllabus (Fall 2026)

Instructor: Dr. A. Rao
Office hours: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 3:00–4:30 PM, Room B-214
Course website: posted on the campus learning portal

## Grading
- Weekly problem sets: 25%
- Two programming projects: 30% (15% each)
- Midterm exam: 20% (Week 8, in class, closed book, one A4 cheat sheet allowed)
- Final exam: 25% (cumulative)

## Late policy
Each student has 3 "grace days" for the whole semester. After grace days are used,
late work loses 10% per day and is not accepted more than 3 days late.

## Weekly schedule
- Week 1: What is machine learning? Supervised vs. unsupervised learning
- Week 2: Linear regression and gradient descent
- Week 3: Logistic regression and classification metrics
- Week 4: Overfitting, regularization (L1 and L2), and cross-validation
- Week 5: Decision trees and random forests
- Week 6: Support vector machines
- Week 7: Neural networks and backpropagation
- Week 8: Midterm exam
- Week 9: Unsupervised learning: k-means clustering and PCA
- Week 10: Introduction to deep learning and CNNs
- Week 11: Natural language processing and embeddings
- Week 12: Large language models and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
- Week 13: ML ethics, bias, and fairness
- Week 14: Project presentations and final review

## Academic integrity
You may discuss ideas with classmates, but all submitted code and writing must be your own.
Using AI tools is allowed for learning, but you must disclose how you used them in each submission.
